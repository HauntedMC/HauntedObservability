# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 3.0.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We are committed to fostering an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of all participants, regardless of background, identity, experience, or status.

## Encouraged Behaviors

Contributors are expected to engage kindly and honestly, respect different viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for their contributions, and help maintain a safe community.

## Restricted Behaviors

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning conduct, unwanted sexualized behavior, disclosure of private information without permission, threats, impersonation, and other conduct that undermines community safety are not acceptable.

## Reporting an Issue

Report conduct concerns privately through HauntedMC support at https://www.hauntedmc.nl/support or GitHub's reporting and moderation tools. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and act according to severity and impact.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when individuals publicly represent the project.
